Find the flats your group can actually agree on.

Everyone independently adds their must-haves, preferences and shortlisted flats. Flat Decider compares them and surfaces the 3 options most worth discussing, with clear tradeoffs.

How it works

  1. Set preferences

    Each person answers privately, marking how much each thing matters.

    Must HavePreferNo Preference

  2. Add flats

    Everyone adds listings they already like: paste a link, or the listing text.

  3. Compare

    Hard rules are checked first, then preferences are matched. Missing details are flagged, never guessed.

  4. Decide together

    Get 3 options with clear tradeoffs. The AI does not make the final decision. Your group does.
